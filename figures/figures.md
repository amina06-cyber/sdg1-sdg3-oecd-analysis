# Figures

This folder contains all figures generated from the analysis.

## Files

### `figure1_scatter.png`
Scatter plot of relative income poverty rate vs infant mortality rate across OECD countries (2021). Includes a fitted linear regression line with 95% confidence interval. Generated using `ggplot2` (R) and `matplotlib` (Python).

### `figure2_trend.png`
Line chart showing the average relative income poverty rate (blue) and average infant mortality rate (red) across all OECD countries from 2000 to 2021. Highlights the divergence between rising poverty and falling infant mortality over time.

### `Rplot3.png`
Quadrant chart classifying all 38 OECD countries into four groups based on whether their poverty rate and infant mortality are above or below the OECD average. Countries are colour-coded by category (high/low poverty × high/low mortality).

### `Rplot4.png`
Scatter plot of relative income poverty rate vs UHC service coverage index across OECD countries. Includes a fitted linear trend line exploring UHC as a moderating factor in the poverty–health relationship.
